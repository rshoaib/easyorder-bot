# orderviachat.com — Site Context

> **URL**: https://orderviachat.com
> **Stack**: Next.js
> **Niche**: Chat-based ordering — let customers place orders via WhatsApp / Messenger / Instagram instead of an app or website
> **Audience**: Restaurant owners, small e-commerce sellers, retail businesses in emerging markets
> **Differentiator**: No customer-side app to install, no friction

## 🎤 Brand Voice

- **Tone**: Operator-friendly, practical, ROI-focused. Like a peer business owner sharing what worked.
- **Style**: Lead with the operator problem. Show before/after order flow. Real numbers where possible.
- **Address**: Second person to a business owner / operator.
- **Values**: Low-friction commerce, meet customers on the channels they already use.

## 🎯 Content Pillars

| Pillar | Topics |
|---|---|
| Restaurant Ops | menu pricing, daily specials, table service, delivery integrations |
| Chat Commerce | WhatsApp Business, Messenger commerce, Instagram Shopping |
| Customer Experience | reducing order friction, repeat orders, loyalty |
| Tech Choice | DIY vs platforms, in-house vs SaaS comparisons |
| Emerging Markets | localization, payment methods (Mada / STC Pay / InstaPay), language UX |
| ROI / Metrics | conversion lift, AOV, customer retention from chat ordering |
