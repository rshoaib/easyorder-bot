# Context — orderviachat.com

## Brand Voice
- **Tone:** Professional yet approachable. Speak to restaurant/shop owners who aren't technical.
- **Persona:** A business consultant who understands small business struggles and WhatsApp culture.
- **Values:** Simplicity, affordability, WhatsApp-native, no technical skills needed.
- **Audience:** Small business owners (restaurants, cafés, shops) in MENA, Latin America, and South Asia.

## Writing Rules
1. Use simple, non-technical language. "Set up your store" not "configure your instance."
2. Focus on business outcomes: more orders, less effort, happier customers.
3. Include real-world examples: "A pizzeria in Dubai increased orders by 40%."
4. Always mention "WhatsApp" — it's the key differentiator.
5. Address the reader as "you" — "Your customers can order in seconds."
