# OrderViaChat — Site Context

> **URL**: https://orderviachat.com
> **Stack**: Next.js + Supabase (multi-tenant SaaS)
> **Revenue**: SaaS subscriptions
> **Core Value**: WhatsApp-first ordering platform for restaurants and small businesses — zero commission, no website needed.

---

## 🎤 Brand Voice

- **Tone**: Professional yet approachable. Speak to restaurant/shop owners who aren't technical.
- **Persona**: A business consultant who understands small business struggles and WhatsApp culture.
- **Style**: Simple, non-technical language. "Set up your store" not "configure your instance."
- **Address**: Second person ("you") — "Your customers can order in seconds."
- **Values**: Simplicity, affordability, WhatsApp-native, no technical skills needed.
- **Audience**: Small business owners (restaurants, cafés, shops) in MENA, Latin America, and South Asia.
- **Differentiator**: Always mention "WhatsApp" and "zero commission" within the first 100 words.
- **Social proof**: Include real-world examples — "A pizzeria in Dubai increased orders by 40%."
